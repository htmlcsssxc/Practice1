Baahubali is a famous Indian epic action film series directed by S.S. Rajamouli that completely changed the standard for grand filmmaking. As noted by Instagram, "Baahubali: The Beginning (2015), directed by S. S. Rajamouli, redefined the scale of Indian epic filmmaking with its breathtaking visuals, grand storytelling, and unforgettable action.” The story follows Shivudu, a strong and brave young man who climbs a massive waterfall and discovers he is the son of the righteous king Amarendra Baahubali from the kingdom of Mahishmati. With amazing battle scenes, rich emotions, and iconic moments like the mystery of Kattappa, the movies became massive box-office hits around the world.
